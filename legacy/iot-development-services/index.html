IoT Development Services

SaM Solutions US understands the complex framework needed to interconnect almost any type of physical product through the Internet. IoT connected devices and machines can improve how we work and live from a smart home or factory to an entire smart city.

What We Do

IoT Cloud-Based Platforms

One of the core components of IoT is a cloud-based platform that makes large-scale data collection and processing possible. This data can then be used to perform actions and send signals to IoT devices. As a leading IoT development services company, SaM Solutions US has the experience required in developing custom IoT/cloud-based platforms.

Edge Computing

Edge computing allows data produced by IoT devices to be processed locally without a need to connect to the Internet. SaM Solutions’ team of IoT experts have decades of combined experience in optimizing design and deployment practices to provide superior IoT development services.

Human-Machine Interfaces & End-User Applications

SaM Solutions can help with developing custom P2M interfaces, control displays, touch panels, interactive user interfaces, and real-time dashboards.

Artificial Intelligence and ML

AI and machine learning are core components of an IoT solution, both of which enable intelligent system behavior. Our experts partner with tech leaders like Amazon (AWS) and Microsoft (Azure) to understand best practices, and also work with open source and custom-built solutions.

IoT Middleware

Connecting all devices and sensors together is one of the most complex parts of IoT. From creating web servers and services, infrastructure libraries, to integrating with external systems and devices. our IoT development services team can do it all.

Firmware for Smart Devices

Developing firmware for IoT devices, kernel porting, and the development/adjustment of modules and drivers for peripheral hardware components have been our core areas of IoT expertise for many years.

FAQ

What is IoT software development?
The Internet of Things (IoT) concept refers to physical objects and devices connected to the network and able to collect and transmit data. IoT software development services include the design, creation, testing, and support of software solutions for such connected devices.
What is an IoT platform?
An IoT platform is a centralized system that allows business owners and employees to monitor all connected assets, analyze collected data, and manage software. IoT platforms can be on-premises or cloud-based. Among the popular IoT platforms are Google Cloud IoT, Microsoft Azure IoT Hub, and Salesforce IoT Cloud.
Is IoT secure?
Security is a critical issue for all IoT providers because all connected devices are vulnerable to cyber threats and hacking. Today, most developers ensure the high security level of IoT systems through strong data encryption, reliable authentication and communication protocols, regular updates, and other advanced practices.
How to choose the right IoT development company?
To choose a development company for your IoT project, start with some market research and select companies that meet your requirements. Try to find recommendations and feedback from their previous clients. Then, arrange meetings with relevant candidates for further discussions of technical and financial details.
