To Optimize Digital Sales, Tender Corporation Picks Magento

  • IndustryRetail, Consumer Goods
  • Solution Magento

Challenge

Tender Corporation started facing difficulties with performance and functionality on the existing web site. Because of the complex adaptability and scalability.

Tender Corporation needed to partner with a team of eCommerce experts that could help identify the best practices for choosing the technology and making it best possible for their needs. The company selected SaM Solutions, and together they picked Magento as the enterprise eCommerce solution to replace the existing web site.

Tender Corporation was founded in 1973 (Littleton, NH), and manufactures the world-famous insect bite treatment, After Bite®, a full line of well-known insect repellents such as Ben's® and Natrapel®, first-aid such as Adventure Medical Kits® and Easy Care First Aid™ kits, survival products with Survive Outdoors Longer®, and burn remedy products such as AfterBurn®.

Solution

SaM Solutions was able to assemble a team of Magento experts that initiated an analysis of the current state of the website. They were able to quickly identify key functions in the legacy website that were missing or lagging in performance. The team was also able to identify key opportunities to enhance the customer experience. New Magento solution functionality included the following:

  • Updated user experience
  • Address mobile strategy growth
  • Responsive design for a desktop and mobile website versions
  • The blog with targeted articles
  • Integrating recommended extensions

Live Magento Developed Solution: www.tendercorp.com

Toolbox

Programming Languages
PHP, JavaScript
Technologies

Success

With the help of the SaM Solutions eCommerce Magento team, Tender Corporation successfully expanded the website functionality and product lines without compromising performance. The Customer Experience significantly improved giving consumers the ability to quickly find the right products and related materials.
