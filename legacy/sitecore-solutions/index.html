FAQ

Why is Sitecore a powerful platform for digital experiences?
Sitecore is a powerful digital IT solution for creating, managing, and customizing digital experiences because it offers robust content management, marketing automation, ecommerce, and analytics features.
What is Sitecore used for?
Many mid-sized businesses, agencies, and large enterprises alike use Sitecore to deliver highly personalized omnichannel experiences that boost customer loyalty, raise revenue, and help outperform competitors.
What is a Sitecore solutions partner?
A Sitecore solutions partner is any company that covers a wide range of Sitecore-related services for multiple Sitecore products and is registered in the Sitecore Partner Network (SPN). Companies that mainly focus on development are often called Sitecore implementation partners.
