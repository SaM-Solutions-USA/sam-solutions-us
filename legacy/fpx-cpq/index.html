FAQ

What is FPX CPQ software?
FPX CPQ is a cloud-based, platform-agnostic system that automates the configuration, pricing, and quoting process for manufacturing companies. Using FPX CPQ, sales reps quickly create accurate quotations of complex configurable products, thus reducing the length of sales cycles.
What are the benefits of FPX CPQ software?
FPX CPQ is a powerful tool used by companies to offer their clients a detailed view of configuration and pricing options for complex products. This way, they can boost the sales operations efficiency and create more sales opportunities resulting in higher revenues. Thanks to FPX CPQ, customers get accurate quotes of customized products instantly.
