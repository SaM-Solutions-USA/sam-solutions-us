Our Sitecore Integration Process

As your Sitecore integration partner, SaM Solutions follows these integration project steps:

1

Integration planning

Together with you, we decide on the integrations that align with your business goals – and create an integration plan that includes a list of necessary tools and a risk mitigation strategy.

2

(Optional) Custom integration planning

If during the previous stage we uncover solutions that require custom APIs for a successful integration with your Sitecore instance, we create additional plans for custom API development.

3

Performing integration

SaM Solutions’ Sitecore team leverages the latest technologies to seamlessly connect all your siloed data points into a cohesive digital ecosystem.

4

Integration testing and maintenance

Our Sitecore team optimizes and tweaks integrations to ensure that all your systems communicate with each other as intended.

FAQ

Why choose SaM Solutions as a Sitecore integration company?
SaM Solutions has been on the global IT market since 1993. We have a full-fledged team of Sitecore experts (including certified Sitecore professionals), led by a four-time Sitecore MVP in Technology.
Can I integrate Sitecore with my custom software?
Technically, yes. However, not every Sitecore integration firm can offer this service as it implies having specialized talents on board. At SaM Solutions, we’re up to the task and have skilled experts ready to develop custom APIs for custom software integration.

Moreover, we don’t only offer, say, custom ecommerce integration with Sitecore but also with Hybris (SAP Commerce), Magento, Salesforce Commerce, Adobe Experience Manager, etc. In other words, we can integrate your custom software with any solution of your choice.
How long does Sitecore CMS integration typically take?
The duration of an integration process varies heavily and depends on the number of planned integrations, their complexity, and the type of software you want Sitecore to be integrated with (out-of-the-box or custom software).
