FAQ

What is Magento?
Magento, currently known as Adobe Commerce, is an eCommerce platform for developing B2B and B2C software solutions. With Magento, you can build multichannel and multilingual eCommerce experiences and seamlessly integrate with third-party services.
What programming language does Magento use?
Magento is written in the PHP programming language.
What is Magento eCommerce development?
This is the development of online stores based on the Magento eCommerce platform. With Magento, online merchants get powerful tools for marketing, content management, sales, and data analytics.
Do you provide support and maintenance services for Magento-based stores?
Yes, we provide ongoing Magento support and maintenance services to take care of your online stores and ensure their trouble-free operation.
